Tilt Graph

A trading journal kept as a graph. It links every position to what came before it, tests which habits really cost money, and checks its own advice against what happened next.

Does the guard's advice hold up

The rule was learned on the first half of every wallet's history (it came out as: average_down). On the second half, every position that reached the same decision got a verdict: WAIT where the habit was followed, GO where it was not. Advice and outcome sit side by side in the graph as (:Position)-[:GUARD_SAID]->(:Verdict). The guard does not claim more than this table shows.
